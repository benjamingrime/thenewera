## Intro

I'm going to give you the conceptual tools to understand how it's posisble that a machine can do intellectual labour and why they will due to market pressures.

To get there, I want to build you a picture of the progress I and others have seen for the last few years.

LLMs have been making progress absurdly fast. We measure these capabilities through *benchmarks*. These are tests, much like (sometimes exactly like) how we measure people's capabilities through standardised tests or qualifications like A-Levels. We track AI's improvements on these benchmarks over time to see how things are progressing.

For example, you might have heard of the Turing Test, proposed in 1949 by Alan Turing (from Manchester by the way, which Mancunians love to mention). This was a benchmark evaluating a machine's ability to exhibit human-level intelligence. A human judge engages in a text-based conversation with both a human and a machine. The judge's task is to determine which participant is human and which is the machine. If the judge cannot reliably distinguish between the human and the machine, the machine is said to have passed the Turing Test. It was originally called the imitation game for obvious reasons.

Up to about three years ago, this was the holy grail of benchmarks and was the talk of the town. Today, no one talks about the Turing Test. LLMs blew right past it.

When ChatGPT launched in November 2022, it could barely do GCSE maths. I still remember where I was when I used an LLM for the first time: I asked about my third-year calculus assignment I was doing, just to test it. It was useless. But still, I was having a conversation with a machine. Six months later, it passed the Bar and got top A-level results, then another six months and it surpassed PhD-level experts in the sciences. A year later, it won gold at the International Maths Olympiad, then the Physics Olympiad, then the IT Olympiad. Today, it does frontier research across all of STEM.

The point is, the raw intellectual capability is here. But how is this possible? How does this integrate with the wider econonmy and become useful?
